# PDF and report output audit, 16 November 2026

Scope: every export either platform produces. Mwangaza: 6 plan reports (ladder, financial independence, school fees, passive income, capital preservation, goals). JiPange: 18 calculator sheets (the household resilience sheet and the three planners were not exportable in this run, see W7). Method: each PDF regenerated from the current build, rasterised at 110 dpi, read page by page, OCR'd (tesseract) for spacing and spelling, and measured for blank space (ink rows, largest vertical gap, bottom margin). Spelling was checked with a general dictionary plus the Kenyan finance vocabulary; British spellings (behavioural, annualised) are house style and were not counted.

OCR on small raster text produces false joins ("Thisis", "Bondladderplan"); those were checked against the page images and are **not** defects. Only what is visible on the page is listed.
