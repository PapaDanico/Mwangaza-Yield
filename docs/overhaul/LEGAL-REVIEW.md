## Found by search (secondary sources, verify before relying)
- ODPC cross-border transfer guidance (2026) treats cloud hosting outside Kenya as a transfer. Publication date is reported inconsistently (draft April, final September); confirm on odpc.go.ke.
- Data Protection (Amendment) Bill 2025: reported as pending; not treated as law.
- CMA enforcement against unlicensed investment platforms (May, Sept 2026); Capital Markets Act s.23 licensing. One source says the "investment adviser" definition now captures robo-advisers; unconfirmed.
