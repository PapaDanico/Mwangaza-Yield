# CBK prospectus practice: how Central Bank of Kenya states the tax on Treasury bond and infrastructure bond interest, 2023-2026

Method note: these were found with WebSearch. CBK PDFs were then fetched with WebFetch, using percent-encoded URLs. The fetch returned the binary, which was saved locally, and the text layer was extracted with `pdftotext -layout`. The quotes marked "verbatim (PDF read)" come from the CBK document itself. The quotes marked "search snippet" come from search-result excerpts of the same CBK URL and have not been read in full. A URL with literal spaces returned 404 from WebFetch; the same file with `%20` encoding worked. So centralbank.go.ke PDFs **were** reachable through WebFetch on 27 Nov 2026, even though `curl` from the sandbox is blocked.

## Q1. What does the "Taxation" line say on recent FXD prospectuses, and is the 10%/15% threshold set by original tenor or by remaining term?

### Takeaway
On every FXD prospectus examined (2023 to Nov 2026), CBK prints 15% for bonds of 2-, 3- and 5-year **original** tenor and 10% for 10-year-and-longer **original** tenor. CBK keeps 10% on long bonds re-opened with as little as 3.0 years to maturity, so in CBK's practice the threshold is the **original tenor**, not the remaining term. The wording changed from a prose "Taxation" line (2023 to early 2025) to a "WITHHOLDING TAX" row in a terms table (2025-2026). Neither form ever states the rule in words; it is always a rate printed per issue.

No 6- to 9-year original-tenor FXD was found that would test the press version directly.

### Inferences
- **Original tenor governs in CBK practice.** CBK prints 10% on re-opened or tapped 10-, 15-, 20- and 30-year bonds with 3.0, 3.2, 6.4, 7.3, 8.3 and 8.7 years left. It prints 15% only on 1-, 2-, 3- and 5-year original-tenor bonds. CBK's own header labels the bond by original tenor first ("Ten Years (3.0 yrs to maturity)"). The statute's "bearer bonds with a maturity of ten years and above" does not say whether maturity means original or remaining. CBK, as paying agent, consistently reads it as original tenor.
- To code: `rate = IFB ? 0 : (originalTenorYears >= 10 ? 10 : 15)`. Take original tenor from the issue code's last segment (e.g. FXD4/2019/**010**), not from the years remaining. This matches the repository's `determineWHTRate` description ("15% below ten years, 10% at ten or more, 0% for IFBs"). The calculation must key on original tenor, never on time to maturity.
- WHT is computed on the clean price (on the discount) and on coupons; the settlement price is dirty price plus WHT. Every prospectus examined repeats the clean-price line.
- The statutory 10% sits in para 5(b)(iii). The note in CLAUDE.md (Rev. 2017 lacks the 10%-at-ten-years rule) does not hold for the 1 July 2026 revision, which carries it.

### Gaps
- I found no CBK or KRA text that states in words that the threshold is original rather than remaining tenor; this is inferred from consistent practice.
- No FXD with an original tenor of 6-9 years was found in 2023-2026, so the 9-to-10-year boundary (FAQ: "up to 9 years") is not tested by a prospectus.
- Whether Act No. 19 of 2026 (outstanding, not applied in the Kenya Law copy) amends para 5 was not checked.

## Q2. What does the taxation line say on recent IFB prospectuses?

### Takeaway
Every IFB prospectus examined states the bond is tax-free. In 2023-2024 the wording was "The Bond will be tax free as is the case for Infrastructure Bonds as provided for under the income Tax Act". In 2025-2026 the table reads "WITHHOLDING TAX N/A", with the line "The bond does not attract withholding tax". None of these prospectuses gives an issue-specific variation for IFBs.

- IFB: 0% for any IFB code. The para 51 condition (listed, maturity of at least 3 years) is satisfied by every IFB seen. The "maturity" there is again ambiguous, but CBK prints N/A even on IFBs re-opened with 5.8 years left, and none was seen with under 3 years left.
- One CBK document was dated 17 Aug 2026 and the Kenya Law consolidation is as at 1 July 2026. A change from Finance Act 2026 could postdate both; the Aug 2026 and Nov 2026 prospectuses show no change in practice.

### Gaps
- I found no IFB prospectus that addresses a non-resident holder or a corporate holder specifically.
- The 2023 IFB wording was not read in the primary documents.

## Q3. Do 2025-2026 prospectuses show a changed rate, or a note on amortisation affecting tax?

### Takeaway
No change appears. The 2025-2026 FXD prospectuses print 10% on every long bond and 15% on 5-year bonds and T-bills (Nov 2026 switch); IFBs print N/A. Amortisation appears only as redemption structure and pledge mechanics. No prospectus says amortisation changes the tax treatment.

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2026 switch: T-bills and the 5-yr FXD1/2023/005 printed at 15%, and the 10-yr FXD4/2019/010 at 10%. — [CBK switch prospectus, Nov 2026](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/treasury_bonds_prospectuses/1533456351_NOVEMBER%202026%20SWITCH%20FROM%20FXD1-2023-005,%202696-091,%202657-182,%202585-364%20TO%20FXD4-2019-010%20DATED%2011-11-2026%20REVISED.pdf)
- Aug 2024 IFB amortisation text. Verbatim (PDF read): "Amortization : Where the borrower does not cancel a pledge contract five (5) days before the amortization date, the amortized bond principal and respective coupon payment, and the balance portion of the bond principal after amortization will be released to the lender." This concerns pledges, not tax. — [CBK prospectus, Aug 2024](https://centralbank.go.ke/uploads/treasury_bonds_prospectuses/718912010_AUG%202024%20IFB1-2023-6.5%20AND%20IFB1-2023-017%20DATED%2019-AUG%202024.pdf)
- IFB1/2024/8.5: "Any amounts up to Kshs. 1.0 million per CSD account at armotization will be redeemed in full." — [CBK prospectus, Feb 2024](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/treasury_bonds_prospectuses/2087959385_February%202024%20IFB1-2024-8.5%20%20DATED%2019-02-2024.pdf)
- KPMG published a 2026 alert, "Kenya Gazettes the Income Tax (Withholding Tax) Rules 2026" (title only; not read). — [KPMG Kenya tax alert, 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/ke/pdf/thought_leaderships/tax/2026/Tax%20Alert%20-%20Kenya%20Gazettes%20the%20Income%20Tax%20(Withholding%20Tax)%20Rules%202026.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)

### Inferences
- The T-bill rows in CBK's own Nov 2026 switch prospectus print "15%". This is primary CBK evidence against the "T-bills are tax-exempt for individuals" claim the repository declined to adopt.
- Amortisation does not change the rate, but the IFB1/2024/8.5 rule that holdings up to KES 1.0m are redeemed in full at the first amortisation is a cash-flow rule worth coding.

### Gaps
- The Withholding Tax Rules 2026 (KPMG alert) and the Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 19 of 2026) were not read. Whether they touch interest on government securities is unconfirmed.

## Q4. What does the CBK/DhowCSD investor FAQ say, and is there any KRA notice?

### Takeaway
CBK's GoK Securities FAQ, an older undated document, states the 15% / 10% (at 10 years or more) / IFB-exempt rule in words. It also says exempt investors must present a KRA tax exemption certificate. I did not locate a DhowCSD-specific FAQ text or a KRA practice note on government securities.

### Cited Findings
- Verbatim (PDF read), FAQ T-bill section: "Note: An investor will be exempt from paying withholding Tax on presentation of Tax Exemption Certificate from the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)." Its worked example applies 15% WHT to a 91-day bill. — [CBK FAQ on GoK Securities](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/images/FAQs/Frequently%20Asked%20Questions%20on%20GoK%20Securities.pdf)
- Bond section of the same FAQ: the verbatim passage is quoted under Q1. — [CBK FAQ on GoK Securities](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/images/FAQs/Frequently%20Asked%20Questions%20on%20GoK%20Securities.pdf)
- KRA publishes a "Withholding Income Tax" guide dated 8/11/2023 (surfaced by search; not read). — [KRA Withholding Income Tax PDF](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Withholding-Income-Tax_8112023.pdf); also [KRA Withholding Tax Overview](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Withholding_Tax_Overview.pdf)

### Inferences
- The FAQ's "tenor" plainly means the bond's tenor as issued. That agrees with the prospectus practice in Q1.

### Gaps
- No DhowCSD FAQ text on tax was retrieved.
- The KRA guides were not read, so whether KRA states original versus remaining tenor is unknown.
- The FAQ is undated and predates DhowCSD (2023), so it may not reflect current wording.
