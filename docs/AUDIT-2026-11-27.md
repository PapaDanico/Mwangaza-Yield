# Audit: Mwangaza Yield and JiPange, 27 November 2026

## Scope, and what was deliberately not done

Bounded to what changed since the last three audits, plus measurements of
both live sites. CLAUDE.md records that a fourth repository-wide sweep
produces confirmations, not findings, so there was none. Covered:

- the code shipped on 27 Nov (#477 to #481 here, JiPange #305 to #310),
  read against its failure modes;
- every route of both live sites at 360px (29 on Mwangaza, 36 on JiPange):
  console errors, failed requests, horizontal overflow;
- page weight after load on both sites;
- layout shift on first and returning visits.

Every finding below was measured, fixed with a test or guard, and the fix was
re-measured. Nothing is listed as a recommendation without a fix behind it.
